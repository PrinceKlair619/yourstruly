Behind the photos

What goes into each project

Kitchens

Most of our kitchens are full remodels: new cabinets and islands, countertops, tile backsplash, lighting, flooring and appliance hookups. We can keep your layout or open it up, and we coordinate every trade so you're not managing plumbers, electricians and installers separately.

Bathrooms

From half baths to primary suites, we handle demolition, plumbing, tile, glass, vanities and fixtures. Walk-in showers, statement tile and modern black or brass finishes are popular requests, and we'll help you pick materials that suit the room and your budget.

Interiors & exteriors

Beyond kitchens and baths we refinish floors, rebuild staircases, finish basements, and repair or rebuild decks, porches, siding and roofs. Many of these jobs start as a repair and become a full upgrade once we've seen the space together.
