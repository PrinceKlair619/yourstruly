About the company

Building in Westchester since 2012

Yours Truly Contracting Group is a licensed and insured general contractor based in Yonkers, New York. Since 2012 we've been turning tired spaces into brand-new ones for homeowners, property owners and businesses across Westchester, Rockland, Long Island and the five boroughs.

Brand-new remodels are at the heart of what we do. Kitchen remodels are a specialty, from new cabinets, countertops and islands to full gut-and-rebuild layouts, and our bathroom remodels range from refreshed half baths to fully tiled walk-in showers and primary suites. Additions, decks and everyday repairs are all part of the job too.

"Yours truly" is how you sign something you stand behind, and that's how we treat every job, whether it's a single leaking pipe or a full gut renovation. You work with one company from the first walkthrough to the final clean-up, with a clear written price before anything starts.
